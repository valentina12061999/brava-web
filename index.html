The challenge

Your customers love you once.
Then they forget you.

Winning a new customer keeps getting harder and more expensive, yet many brands still treat the first purchase as the finish line. Without a thoughtful lifecycle strategy, customers drift away, email turns into a discount channel and you keep paying to replace people you’ve already won.

01

Acquisition keeps getting more expensive.

02

You’re not getting enough from your customer base.

Your list keeps growing, but your emails aren’t driving enough repeat revenue beyond promotions.

03

The journey isn’t built to bring people back.

Customers buy and receive their order. What happens next determines whether they come back.

Why retention

You already did the hard part.
You got them to buy.

Every new customer represents money spent on media, creative, content and the work it takes to earn their attention.
Retention is about making that first purchase worth more, so you don’t have to start from zero every time.

  • More repeat purchases
  • More value from every customer
  • A healthier balance between acquisition and growth

What I do

One growth system, built around retention.

Most brands put their energy into getting the next customer. I look at what happens after the click: the first purchase, the product experience and the decision to come back. Then I build the journeys, messaging and touchpoints that make the next order an easy yes.

About

Hi, I’m Valentina

Photo 1:1

I’m a digital marketer with a soft spot for brands that care about what they create.

I’ve spent over five years working in advertising agencies, helping brands grow through paid media and digital marketing. And I kept noticing the same thing: brands put more and more into winning new customers, but far less into what happens after the first order.

That’s where I saw the opportunity. I started Brava Growth Studio to help independent ecommerce brands in beauty, wellness and fashion get more value from the customers they’ve worked so hard to win, turning first orders into repeat purchases and long-term customer relationships.

Brava means brave in Catalan and Spanish. It’s a nod to where I’m from, and a reminder that good growth takes the courage to question what isn’t working and build something better.

My background is in acquisition. My focus now is what happens next.

Why work with me

  • I’ve worked across the full funnel. From paid social and Google Ads to broader digital strategy, I’ve seen how brands win customers and what it really takes to get them through the door.
  • I understand acquisition economics. I’ve seen first-hand what brands spend to win a new customer. Retention is how you get more value from every dollar already spent.
  • You work directly with me. No account managers, no layers. The person you speak to is the person doing the work, from our first call to the final optimisation.

Sydney-based, globally minded. Working with brands in English, Spanish and Catalan.
